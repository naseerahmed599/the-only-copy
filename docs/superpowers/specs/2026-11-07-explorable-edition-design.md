# The Only Copy: the explorable edition

Date: 7 November 2026. Status: agreed in conversation; building.

## Why

She asked him, in a car, to read her favourite book. Three days later she said she
would not talk to him until he had. From home she sent a photo of her shelf: thirty
editions of The Little Prince in about twenty languages. The current page proves he
read it. This revision makes it answer "I heard you": his voice present throughout,
the book explorable rather than only scrolled, real photographs under one palette,
and an ending that asks for her reply in the book's own terms.

## Goals

- Stays by the book. Chapter order, events and the sense of the known lines are kept.
  It is a reading in his voice, not a quotation.
- Explorable. Every chapter offers one thing to find or do; nothing blocks the scroll.
- Visually richer. Six to eight freely licensed photographs (Wikimedia Commons, CC0 or
  public domain first) for scenes with a real-world subject, treated so they read as
  one night. His own two AI images (Milky Way, Cottbus rooftops) stay. Procedural art
  stays everywhere else.
- Warm, inspiring, poetic. Romantic only by restraint: one warm line, nothing she has
  to pretend she did not read.

## Non-goals and anti-goals

- No link to Instagram or any app. The ending asks for her reply in words only.
- No name, no birthday wish before 3 December, no fox/rose/taming lines aimed at her,
  no prefilled replies, no counters or game HUD, no photoreal images presented as
  evidence of real events.
- No rewrite. The existing scroll, GSAP choreography, URL and QR all stay.

## The parts

1. **Exact shelf.** Thirty spines transcribed from her photo drive the constellation;
   the dedication, epilogue and colophon say thirty and thirty-first.
2. **The opening.** Before the dedication, a short scene in his voice: the car, the
   question, "I will not talk to you until you read it", so I read it.
3. **Hidden notes.** A small ✎ star in each chapter opens one line from him. Plain,
   true, short. Listed for him to approve before sending.
4. **Photographs.** Desert (day, night), wheat, fennec, well, baobab, roses, Earth at
   night, railway. One CSS treatment: slight desaturation, mood tint through
   mix-blend-mode, existing grain. CREDITS.txt lists file, author, licence, source;
   linked from the colophon in the smallest type.
5. **The pocket.** Five drawn objects appear quietly in the margin as she passes them
   (the sheep's box, the rose under glass, the fox's secret folded small, a cup of well
   water, one bell). No counter. At the shelf they rise and become the thirty-first star.
6. **The ending.** After the colophon, two light questions only a reader could ask,
   asked poetically, with nothing to tap: what her Czech copy says about the sunsets,
   and which chapter is hers.

## Left to the builder

Exact placement of photographs per scene, the drawing of the five pocket objects,
tap-target sizes (44 px minimum at 390 px), reduced-motion fallbacks, and the order
of pushes so the live URL is never broken.
